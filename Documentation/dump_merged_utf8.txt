  TEXT: "Small food businesses like cafes, bakeries, independent restaurants make daily purchasing decisions with no reliable way to predict tomorrow's demand."
  TEXT: 'The core disconnect: a cafe owner does not think in RMSE or MAPE — they want to know exactly how many loaves of bread, croissants, or cups of coffee to order tomorrow or make. Across the forecasting literature we reviewed, most studies (Aqila et al., Jatte et al., Gartner et al., Susanto et al.) stop at computing an error metric and never take the final step of producing a purchase quantity.'
  TEXT: 'Forecast-to-Action Gap'
  TEXT: 'Predictions rarely convert into an operational restock quantity.'
  TEXT: 'Accessibility Gap'
  TEXT: 'Accurate tools assume enterprise dashboards and IT staff.'
  TEXT: 'Infrastructure Gap'
  TEXT: 'Best-performing systems assume IoT hardware or heavy compute.'
  TEXT: 'Cold-Start Gap'
  TEXT: 'Deep learning models fail on the short, sparse histories real SMEs.'

  TEXT: 'MUKESH PATEL SCHOOL OF TECHNOLOGY MANAGEMENT & ENGINEERING, SHIRPUR'
  TEXT: '20'
  TEXT: 'Feasibility of Implementation'
  TEXT: 'Where the Approach Is Strong'
  TEXT: "•  Facebook Prophet: no GPU, minimal tuning, native seasonality & holiday support — configuration, not custom code\n•  LSTM considered, not chosen — needs 1,000+ days of history we don't have\n•  XGBoost alone was ruled out weak on sparse SME data (Susanto et al.); as a Prophet residual-corrector it's proven (Kamboj et al., 12–18% MAPE gain)  this is what we use\n•  Restock logic is deterministic Pandas/NumPy — engineering work, not a research problem"
  TEXT: 'Where This Creates an Opening'
  TEXT: '•  No reviewed study combines a Prophet + XGBoost hybrid, 3-phase cold-start, restock-quantity conversion, waste-action markdowns, and a zero-setup mobile interface with plain-language explanation in one system\n•  It is positioned to fill that specific combined gap for small food businesses'
  TEXT: "Where We're Stretched Thin"
  TEXT: '•  No real business data yet  public datasets (French Bakery, Rossmann) used as proxies\n•  11-week technical build compressed across 3 people running parallel workstreams\n•  The weather regressor is the one genuinely custom integration in the pipeline'
  TEXT: 'What Could Work Against Us'
  TEXT: "•  Weather API rate limits on the free tier could reduce regressor reliability at scale\n•  Public-dataset proxies may not fully represent a real cafe/bakery's demand pattern accuracy may not transfer 1:1 to live deployment"
