Introduction
Small food businesses lose money on both sides of the same problem: over-order and waste perishable stock, or under-order and lose sales with no forecasting tool built for their scale.
THE PROBLEM
Over- and under
-
ordering drives food waste and lost revenue for small operators, with no forecasting tool built for their scale.
WHY IT PERSISTS
AI-driven forecasting has matured for large retail chains, but remains inaccessible to small operators without dedicated IT or data teams.
THE 
TRADITIONAL 
APPROACH
Small operators currently rely on manual spreadsheets, simple moving averages, or pure gut-feel. These static methods completely fail to account for dynamic demand shocks like sudden weather changes or public holidays.

Problem Statement
Small food businesses like cafes, bakeries, independent restaurants make daily purchasing decisions with no reliable way to predict tomorrow's demand.
The core disconnect: 
a cafe owner does not think in RMSE or MAPE — they want to know exactly how many loaves of bread, croissants, or cups of coffee to order tomorrow or make. Across the forecasting literature we reviewed, most studies (Aqila et al., Jatte et al., Gartner et al., Susanto et al.) stop at computing an error metric and never take the final step of producing a purchase quantity.
Forecast-to-Action Gap
Predictions rarely convert into an operational restock quantity.
Accessibility Gap
Accurate tools assume enterprise dashboards and IT staff.
Infrastructure Gap
Best-performing systems assume IoT hardware or heavy compute.
Cold-Start Gap
Deep learning models fail on the short, sparse histories real SMEs.

Proposed Methodology
An end-to-end architecture that turns raw sales data into both a restock quantity and a waste-reduction action, explained in plain language.
Historical Sales Data
Daily item-level sales (bakery dataset)
Cold-Start Handling
Day 1–13: category priors
Day 14–30: blended
Day 30+: personal model
NEW
Prophet + XGBoost
Prophet: trend + seasonality
XGBoost: residual correction only
Hybrid Forecast
Corrected point prediction, per item, per day
Restock Logic
Safety-stock buffer + current inventory
Recommendation
e.g. “Order 15 croissants”
Inventory Aging Check
Flags stock nearing sell-by or low turnover
Waste Action Engine
Uses price-elasticity data to size the discount
Waste Action
e.g. “Mark down 8 croissants 30% today”
NEW — WASTE PATH
Forecast Narrator (LLM)   
Translates the restock quantity and the waste action into plain-language advice for a non-technical owner narration only, never touches the forecasting math.

=== Slide 17 ===
17
Challenges Encountered
Real issues hit during implementation so far, plus the current active blocker.
Validation Methodology Gap
Standard k-fold cross-validation leaks future data into training for a time series. Pivoted to strict chronological walk-forward (rolling-origin) validation instead.
Data Sparsity / Zero-Sale Days
Frequent zero-sale days in the SME bakery data broke standard MAPE, producing misleading error values. Pivoting to SMAPE / RMSE for sparse-data evaluation.
Untuned Hyperparameters
changepoint_prior_scale is still inherited from an early baseline. Grid-search bounds for Prophet tuning on the bakery-specific data are not yet finalized.
Current Blocker: Backend Not Yet Built
The FastAPI layer and the restock_qty / waste_action JSON contract with backend still need to be defined before Flutter ↔ MongoDB integration can begin.
